Privacy Policy

Effective October 16, 2026

Pingo is a personal, non-commercial prototype operated by Nicholas Brueggeman, an individual in Yuma, Arizona ("I", "me"). This policy explains what information Pingo handles for its invited testers and how.

Text messaging and your mobile number

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Information sharing with subcontractors that support the service, such as the messaging provider that delivers texts, is permitted. All other categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

Messages are recurring and conversational. Message frequency varies with how you use Pingo. Message and data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help and STOP to opt out at any time; after STOP you receive one confirmation and no further messages unless you reply START.

What Pingo stores

  • Your name, mobile number, your opt-in record (date, time and the consent text you agreed to), and opt-out status.
  • Your conversations with Pingo and the notes Pingo keeps to help you (preferences, reminders, tasks).
  • If you choose to connect them: information from your own health-plan or healthcare accounts, and the access tokens or credentials needed to reach them, stored only for your own assistant.

Each tester's assistant runs on its own isolated server. Nobody else's assistant can read your information.

Who processes it

To run Pingo, your messages and data are processed by these service providers acting on my behalf: Fly.io (hosting), Twilio (text messages and calls), Telegram (if you use Pingo on Telegram) and OpenAI (the AI model that generates replies). I do not sell your information or use it for advertising, and I do not train AI models on it.

Pingo is a personal prototype, not a service of a health plan or healthcare provider. No HIPAA business associate agreements are in place with these providers, so please share only your own information and only what you are comfortable sharing.

Keeping and deleting your information

Your information is kept while you are a tester. You can ask me to delete everything at any time by texting "delete my data" to Pingo or emailing me. Deletion removes your assistant's server and everything on it, your records in Pingo's systems, and your message history at the messaging provider where it allows deletion.

Security

Information is encrypted in transit. Credentials are stored as secrets on your assistant's server and are never included in messages. No system is perfectly secure; I will tell affected testers promptly about any breach of their information.

Eligibility

Pingo is for invited adults 18 and over.

Changes and contact

I will update this page and the effective date if this policy changes, and text testers about material changes. Questions or requests: r4di4ti0n@gmail.com.
