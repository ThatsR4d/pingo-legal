Pingo

An AI assistant service by Nicholas Brueggeman (sole proprietor). Early access.

Pingo is an automated AI assistant you can text. After you sign up, the Pingo service helps you with everyday tasks and with your own health-plan and healthcare questions, such as rides to medical appointments, food assistance programs, coverage renewals and your own health records, and it sends you the reminders you set up.

Every text from Pingo is generated and sent by the Pingo software to the person who signed up. Pingo does not relay messages between people, and it sends no marketing, promotions or third-party content. Pingo is in early access: sign-ups are activated as capacity allows.

Text messages from Pingo: recurring automated messages: replies to your requests, reminders you set, and account notifications such as secure sign-in links. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help or STOP to opt out at any time.
