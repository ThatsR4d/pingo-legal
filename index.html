Pingo

A personal, non-commercial prototype by Nicholas Brueggeman

Pingo is a personal AI assistant you can text. It is a small private test run by one individual developer for a handful of invited adult testers. It helps testers with everyday tasks and with their own health-plan and healthcare questions, such as rides to appointments, food assistance programs, coverage renewals and their own health records, and it sends reminders a tester asks for.

Pingo is invite-only. There is no marketing, no promotions and no third-party content.

Text messages from Pingo: recurring, conversational messages in reply to your requests and reminders you set. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help or STOP to opt out at any time.
